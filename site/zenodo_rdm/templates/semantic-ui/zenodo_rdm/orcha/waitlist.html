Help us test the new AI-assisted deposit form

As part of the AIRDEC project, we are developing and testing new AI-assisted features for the Zenodo deposit form. These features are designed to make depositing research outputs easier and faster by automatically extracting and suggesting metadata from uploaded PDF files.

We are looking for 100 users to test the new features and provide feedback based on their experience.

Opening and testing period
Opening date: 30 October 2026
Testing period: Until February 2027

Our goal is to make the deposit process approximately 50% faster by reducing the amount of manual metadata entry required.

What is being tested?
The new AI-assisted functionality will provide automated metadata extraction and suggestions for the following fields in the deposit form:"

  • Title
  • Description
  • Copyright
  • License
  • Authors
  • Publication date
  • Funding / Grants

The testing will be available for PDF files only.

The AI integration will analyse the uploaded PDF and provide suggestions for the relevant metadata fields. You can then review, accept, modify, or replace these suggestions before completing your deposit.

How to provide feedback
You will have the opportunity to provide feedback in the comments box close to the AI suggestion. Providing feedback is optional, but we will be grateful if you could help us in identifying incorrect suggestions, missing information, or aspects that could be improved. SCREENSHOT COMMENT BOX

At the end of the form, you can also provide additional comments about your overall experience.

How is my data used by AI?
For details on how AI is used across Zenodo, please see our dedicated AI usage notice page.

Your feedback matters
Your feedback will help us evaluate the quality, usefulness, speed, and usability of the new AI-assisted deposit features and identify areas for improvement before further development and rollout.

Thank you for helping us improve Zenodo !
